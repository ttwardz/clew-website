Pricing

Clew is free to download, and the core features of the app will remain free. That includes unlimited highlight creation, unlimited sources, unlimited tags, and much more.

However, Clew is not free to develop and maintain for me—just one person building this app. So, in the future, some advanced features and customizations will require a ✨ Clew Deluxe subscription.
