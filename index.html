Why Choose Clew?

Clew is the ultimate app for storing and exploring your reading highlights, margin notes, and other insights from books, articles, and other media. Here's why you'll love Clew:

  • Effortlessly capture and organize your highlights and notes from various sources.
  • Tag and categorize your insights to quickly find what you need.
  • Sync your data across multiple devices, so your notes are always with you.

Pricing

Clew is free to download, and the majority of its features are and will remain free.

Clew Deluxe is an optional subscription that unlocks several features for power users. With a Clew Deluxe subscription, you get:

  • Highlight Cards
  • Readwise Import
  • Custom Widget Themes

There will continue to be plenty of new features coming to the app for free, as well as additional features for Clew Deluxe users.
