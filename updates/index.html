Updates

Clew 1.5

July 31, 2026

Clew 1.5 is the biggest update yet—a new iPad experience, a redesigned look for sources and highlights, on-device Apple Intelligence, better bulk import options, and a whole lot more.

New features:

A New Way to Navigate on iPad 🖥️

Clew now uses a three-column layout on iPad—collections, your list, and the detail view side by side. With a hardware keyboard you can drive the whole app: arrow keys to move between columns, ⌘F to search from anywhere, ⌘1–8 to jump between sections, ⇧⌘O for Quick Search, and ⌘⌫ to delete.

A Redesigned Look for Sources and Highlights 🎨

Book, song, album, and web page pages have been rebuilt around a color wash drawn from the artwork itself, with a contained jacket layout on iPad. Highlight pages get a source-themed footer, a cleaner card surface, one consistent color hierarchy, and a marker stroke behind the quote that follows the words instead of boxing them.

Get Started Faster 👋

New to Clew? A first-run welcome walks you through the app, with optional starter highlights you can explore and clear out whenever you like.

Built on Apple Intelligence ✨

Clew now uses Apple's on-device foundation models to understand your library. Tag suggestions read what a highlight is actually about to suggest existing and new tags. This runs on device—your highlights never leave it, and no data is shared.

Import from Spreadsheets 📄

Bring in highlights from CSV and XLSX files, with columns matched for you automatically. Imported books are enriched with covers, ISBN, and publication year.

Other new and improved features:

  • Siri, Shortcuts & Spotlight—App Shortcuts and Siri intents (search highlights, quick-save, get highlights by source, Open in Clew), plus full Spotlight indexing of your library.
  • Multi-window support—open highlights in their own window on iPad.
  • Drag and drop highlights between tags and sources.
  • AI tag suggestions for untagged highlights, right in the detail view.
  • Selectable text—select ranges of highlight and note text.
  • Typography—set text size across the whole app, and give the quote on a highlight's page any font installed on your device.
  • Stats: Standouts—record-holder cards for your longest, most-tagged, and most-revisited highlights, plus year-in-review stats.
  • Widgets—song artwork, a new artwork-split layout that pairs the cover art with the quote, and refreshed built-in themes.
  • Streamlined list controls—filter chips and sort menu combined into one row with a sort-direction arrow.
  • Filter by Tag in reach—on iPhone the tag filter moves into the bottom bar beside search on a source's page, so filtering and searching the same list sit together.
  • Delete confirmation now counts the highlights attached to a book, web page, or song, and lets you delete them alongside it or keep them.
  • Faster source attachment—Add Source is one tap into the picker, and source detail pages keep an add-highlight button in reach as you scroll.
  • Better undo/redo descriptions when you shake your device or use three-finger gestures.
  • Book search now defaults to Apple Books everywhere.
  • Copy URL button on web page details.
  • Privacy policy moved to a web-hosted page.

Bug fixes:

  • Fixed a bug where a failed backup restore could damage existing data. Older backups that previously failed to import now restore correctly.
  • Fixed crashes opening results from Quick Search and Spotlight.
  • Fixed editing a book adding a "finished reading" date it never had, and preserved the original date when toggling a book's read status.
  • Fixed restored books losing their read status when a backup recorded a finished date but no read/unread flag.
  • Fixed More From Tags showing the wrong number of highlights on a tag.
  • Fixed cover images being lost or attached to the wrong item.
  • Fixed tag colors not updating in list rows.
  • Fixed landscape photo orientation causing incorrect text recognition.
  • Fixed the keyboard dismissing while editing scanned text chunks during bulk import from photos.

I'd appreciate an App Store rating if you're enjoying Clew. If you have a suggestion or noticed a bug, contact me.

Clew 1.4.5 Update

Check out what's new in Clew!

New features:

Scan Multiple Book Pages 📸

Quickly capture highlights from multiple book pages or documents using the camera. Now, you can finish a chapter—or even a whole book—and then add all your annotations to Clew at once. This includes improved metadata extraction and book auto-selection, better margin detection and page recognition.

On-Device AI Insights (Beta - iOS 26+) ✨

For devices with Apple Intelligence, Clew now discovers meaningful connections in your highlights. Echo Detection finds highlights with similar themes, Cross-Pollination reveals connections across different books and sources, and Tag Evolution shows how your interests develop over time. Your Library, Visualized 📊

A new Stats view shows your reading patterns at a glance. See your most-used tags, track highlight counts over time, and discover insights about your collection through beautiful charts and graphs.

Clew 1.0.6 Update

May 2, 2025

Clew 1.0.6 brings the following enhancements:

  • BACKUP AND RESTORE get new features: Your complete database is already backed up automatically once per day. You can also manually back up at any time. Now, it's easier than ever to completely restore from a backup. Or, choose Compare with Backup to compare your current data with any backup so that you can easily restore specific items if you accidentally deleted something.

  • The add highlight button is now conveniently visible on all views.
  • Widgets are more interactive - you can tap a book title or author name to quickly open that page.
  • You can filter highlights by untagged or tagged only, in addition to showing all.
  • The web search for autofilling new book information has better, more comprehensive results from multiple sources. It also now uses a work's original publication year instead of the specific edition's publication year.
  • When editing a highlight, tapping Add Note will automatically focus the note text editor, and tapping Add Tags will automatically show the select tags sheet.
  • The plus (+) button on widgets is optional now. You can add/remove it by tapping and holding on a widget and choosing Edit Widget.
  • There's a new, convenient Add Source button when you're viewing a highlight that doesn't have one yet.
